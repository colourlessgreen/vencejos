Based in Granada. Working across Madrid, Barcelona, Bilbao, Málaga, Sevilla, Valencia, Oviedo — and the small towns nobody puts on a list.

You built something — a career, a household, a life. So, why is it still hard? You're over 40 and you still feel like you're barely making it. And, the politics. Oh, goth, the politics. Everyone's pissed at each other and you can't even wear a pair of shoes without it being considered a political statement. Step away. Take a breath; that breath is here in Spain. I want you to come to Spain without spending your first year figuring out things a local could tell you in ten minutes. Maybe your kids are out of school now. (Oh, and if they're not out of school yet, don't worry. There are no active shooter drills here, they're better off here.) And you wonder what's next? Do you want adventure? Or do you just want to chill?

And you look around and you see that the quality of life where you are is, well, shit.

As an American who has navigated Spanish life on the ground for three years, I created GTFO — Guiri* Transition for Outsiders — to be your local guide, cultural bridge, and practical safety net. I am thrilled to be here every. single. day.

We leave the formal visa paperwork to licensed legal teams so we can focus entirely on the human side of your move: finding your ideal neighborhood, understanding real living costs, and connecting you directly with the local community. We're here to plug you into life and living. And give you practical advice on how to obtain the basics that your lawyer may have overlooked; from finding a dentist to getting on the public healthcare system.

*Guiri is a Spanish slang term for a foreigner, usually an American or British person. Kinda like gringo in Mexico.
